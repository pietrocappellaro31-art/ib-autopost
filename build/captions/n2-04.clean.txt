You calculate equity value per share and use basic shares. The interviewer's next question is about options.

The treasury stock method. Only count options in the money, with a strike below today's share price.

Say 10 options at a $10 strike, share price $20. Exercising them brings in $100. The method assumes the company uses that $100 to buy back shares at $20, so 5 come back.

Net new shares: 5. Basic 100 becomes 105 diluted.

When you're solving for an implied price, the share count moves with it, which is why models iterate.
