Bankers read cold emails on their phones between calls. Write for that.

Subject line: a real connection plus the ask. Your shared university gets it opened. "Quick question" doesn't.

Body: 4 sentences. Who you are, why them specifically, 1 ask for a 15 minute call, and when you're free.

Keep the ask small. A short call is an easy yes. A referral comes later, after a real conversation.

No reply in a week? 1 short nudge in the same thread. Then move on.
