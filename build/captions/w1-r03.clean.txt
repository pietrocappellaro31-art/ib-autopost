"I want to learn a lot and work with smart people." So does everyone else in that hallway.

"Why investment banking" has 3 parts. Most answers have 1.

Part 1, where it started. One specific moment. A deal you followed, a company you watched get bought, a conversation that sent you down a rabbit hole at 2am. Not "an interest in markets."

Part 2, what you did about it. This is the part that proves part 1 was true. A club you joined, a 3-statement model you built, a stock pitch you wrote, 40 coffee chats. Without it, part 1 is a story.

Part 3, why this seat. Why banking rather than equity research, or consulting, or the fund down the road.

Here's the test, and it takes 10 seconds.

Read your answer back and swap "investment banking" for "consulting." If it still works, you haven't answered the question yet.
