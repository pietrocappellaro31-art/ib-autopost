Swap 1 word in your "Why banking?" answer. If it still works, it fails.

Here's the test. Take your answer and replace "investment banking" with "consulting." Then "tech." Then "law."

"I want a steep learning curve, real responsibility early and smart people around me" survives every swap. That's the problem. If it works for any job, it isn't an answer to this one.

The answers that pass have 3 parts.

1. Where it started. One specific moment, like a deal you followed or a company you watched get bought. Not "an interest in markets."

2. What you did about it. A model you built, a stock pitch, a club you ran. It's the proof that part 1 really happened.

3. Why this seat. Why banking, and not equity research or consulting.

Now try the swap on a specific answer: "Watching Broadcom buy VMware, I wanted to know why a chip company would pay that much for software. So I built the model. Banking is where I'd be on the deal, not reading about it after."

Put "consulting" in there and it breaks. That's what you want.
