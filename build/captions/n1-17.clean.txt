Cost synergies or revenue synergies? Buyers trust 1 of them a lot more.

Cost synergies are what you can cut: a duplicate head office, 2 IT systems running side by side, 2 purchasing teams. Once the deal closes, they're in management's hands.

Revenue synergies are what you hope to sell.

Cross-selling, new markets, bigger contracts. They depend on customers doing something they haven't done yet, which is why boards and lenders lean on cost synergies when they justify a premium.

Neither shows up on day 1. You pay integration costs first, then synergies phase in, part in year 1 and more in year 2, before you hit a full run-rate.

Count what you control.
