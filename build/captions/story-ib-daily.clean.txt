"Walk me through a DCF." He got the offer. Here's the answer he gave, in 5 steps.

1. Project the company's free cash flow, usually 5 years out.
2. Discount it back at WACC.
3. Add a terminal value for everything after year 5, and discount that too.
4. Sum them. That's enterprise value.
5. Bridge to equity value: subtract net debt, then divide by diluted shares if they want a price per share.

That's it. If you can say those 5 lines out loud without looking at your notes, and explain why each one comes in that order, you're ready for this question.
