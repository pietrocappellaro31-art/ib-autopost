Depreciation goes up by 10. Walk me through the three statements.

That's the real question. Anyone can name the three. What separates people is taking one line, changing it, and tracing it through all of them without stopping.

Net income falls 7.5, not 10, because the tax saving is real cash you keep.
Add the depreciation back and cash is up 2.5.
Assets fall 7.5, equity falls 7.5, and it balances.

Say "and it balances" out loud at the end. That's the sentence the interviewer is waiting for, and most people stop one line short of it.

State your tax rate before you start. 25% is fine. Picking one and saying it is part of the answer.

Save this for the night before.
