What makes a good LBO candidate? Every answer comes back to 1 thing: can it pay down the debt?

Stable, predictable cash flow. Lenders lend against cash they can count on, so a cyclical business with lumpy years is a risk.

Low capex. Picture 2 companies with $100 of EBITDA, one spending $10 on capex and one spending $60. The first has $90 to put towards the debt. The second has $40.

Then room to improve, because hoping the exit multiple goes up isn't a plan: costs to cut, assets to sell, margins to raise.

And a clear way out. A strategic buyer, another sponsor, an IPO. You don't have a return until you've sold it.
