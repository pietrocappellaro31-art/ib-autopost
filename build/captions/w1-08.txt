"Tell me about yourself" is the only question you know is coming. Most people still waste it.

90 seconds. 3 beats. One thread running through all of them.

Beat 1, where you started. 20 seconds, not 60. Where you're from, what you study, enough to place you. Then move.

Beat 2 is the only part they'll remember. One decision and what you did about it. A deal you followed, a club you joined, a model you built at 2am because you wanted to know how it worked. This is where a person shows up instead of a candidate.

Beat 3 points at the door. "Which is why I want to be in this seat, at this bank." It hands them the follow-up question you actually want.

Then cut it. A written answer of 220 words is about 90 seconds spoken. Most people deliver over 3 minutes and don't notice, because rehearsing in your head is always faster than saying it out loud.

Time it with a stopwatch. Once.

Save this.
