Prices are rising. LIFO or FIFO? Work it with 3 numbers.

You bought 1 unit at 10, then another at 14, and sold 1 for 20.

FIFO expenses the oldest cost: COGS 10, gross profit 10, tax at 25% is 2.5.
LIFO expenses the newest: COGS 14, gross profit 6, tax 1.5.

So LIFO shows less profit but keeps more cash, because it pays less tax.

FIFO wins on the balance sheet. Inventory sits at 14, closer to what it'd cost to replace. LIFO leaves it at 10.

Last point: IFRS doesn't allow LIFO. US GAAP does. Compare across the two and you adjust first.

Save it for the accounting round.
