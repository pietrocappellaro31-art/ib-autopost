TITLE: Right 99 Days Out of 100. How Do You Still Lose Money?
ALT 1: You Win 99% of the Time and Still Lose. How?
ALT 2: 99 Wins, 1 Loss, and You're Down. How Does That Happen?

DESCRIPTION:
You're right 99 days out of 100 and you still end up down. Here's how, in our own example. You sell insurance against a big market move and collect £1 on every calm day, so after 99 calm days in a row you're up £99 and feeling pretty clever about it. Then on day 100 the market jumps and you pay out £150. Down £51. From 99 wins and one loss.

How often you win isn't the whole story. What you lose when you're wrong decides it. Selling options can work the same way: small, steady wins, then one big loss.
