Your whole DCF rests on 1 number, and most candidates can't defend it.

WACC. Every term, and where each one comes from.

The weights are market value, not book. Equity weight uses market cap. Pulling book equity off the balance sheet is the single most common slip here.

Cost of debt is after tax. 100 of interest at a 25% rate costs the company 75, because the deduction is real. There's no equivalent on equity, since dividends aren't deductible. Haircut both and you've misunderstood why the haircut exists at all.

Cost of equity comes from CAPM. Risk-free rate, plus beta times the equity risk premium.

Beta isn't 1 number off a screen. Take your comps' betas. Strip out each company's use. Average the business risk. Re-lever at the structure you're modelling. That's 4 steps, and interviewers ask for all of them.

And use the current yield, not the coupon. A bond issued at 4% that now yields 6.5% costs you 6.5% to refinance. WACC looks forward.

Save this one.
