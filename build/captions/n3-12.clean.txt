What's an earn-out? Part of the price, paid later, only if the business hits its targets.

It exists because the 2 sides disagree. The seller believes the forecast. The buyer doubts it. The earn-out pays only if it comes true.

You tie it to 1 clear metric, like revenue or EBITDA over 2 or 3 years. Vague targets end in disputes.

Under ASC 805 and IFRS 3 it's contingent consideration, booked at fair value at closing and usually remeasured through profit and loss.

Here's the catch. The seller doesn't run the business anymore, but gets paid on its results, so an earn-out often just delays the argument.
