All-stock deal. Is it accretive? You can answer in 1 line if you compare the right 2 numbers.

Buyer trades at 20 times earnings and pays 15 times for the target, premium included, which means it's swapping expensive paper for cheaper earnings, so EPS goes up.

Flip them. Dilutive.

Cash deal? You compare the after-tax cost of that cash or debt with the target's earnings yield at the price paid. Yield is 1 over the P/E, so at 20 times it's 5%.

Then add the line I'd want to hear: accretive doesn't mean good. Overpay and EPS can still rise while value falls.

Save this one for M&A questions.
