Does growth always add value? Most people say yes. The answer depends on 2 rates.

ROIC is what the business earns on the capital it puts in. WACC is what that capital costs.

Invest 100 at a 12% return when capital costs 10%: you earn 12, pay 10, and keep 2 a year. As a perpetuity at 10%, that's about 20 of value.

Invest the same 100 at 8%: revenue and profit grow, and the company's worth less.

At exactly the cost of capital, growth adds nothing.

So in a DCF, if you raise growth, raise reinvestment too.

Save it for the valuation round.
