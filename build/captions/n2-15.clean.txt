Why can't you value a bank on EV / EBITDA? Because for a bank, debt isn't financing. It's inventory.

Deposits and borrowing are what it lends out, and interest is its cost of sales. You can't split operations from financing, so EV and EBITDA stop meaning anything.

Use equity multiples.

P/E, price to book, price to tangible book. If you want the intrinsic version, it's a dividend discount model.

P/B comes down to ROE against the cost of equity: P/B = (ROE - g) / (COE - g). Plug in an ROE of 12%, a cost of equity of 10% and 4% growth and you get about 1.33.

ROE equal to COE? P/B is 1.0.
