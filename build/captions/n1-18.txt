"Tell me about a time you failed." They're not grading the failure. They're grading what came after it.

"I work too hard" is the real failure here. It tells them you won't own a mistake on a live deal.

Pick something that mattered, that was at least partly your fault, and that didn't end anything.

Own your part in 1 plain sentence. I, not we.

Spend most of the answer on the fix: what you did, and the habit you kept.

Then show it stuck. 1 later moment where you did it right is worth more than "I learned a lot".

What failure would you use?
