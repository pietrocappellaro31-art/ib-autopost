Why would a buyer pay for a target's past losses? Because they can cut future tax.

Net operating losses offset taxable income later. 100 of NOLs at a 25% rate is worth up to 25 of tax saved.

Up to. The company has to earn taxable income to use them, so value them year by year and discount what's saved.

In the US, federal losses from 2018 on don't expire, but each year they can offset at most 80% of taxable income.

After an acquisition, Section 382 caps how much can be used each year: the old company's value times a long-term tax-exempt rate.
