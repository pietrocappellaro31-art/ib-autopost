A comp set isn't a list. It's an argument that the market should value your target exactly the way it values these 8 companies, so every name you include is a claim you have to be ready to defend out loud.

Screen on the business, not the label. Same model, similar size and growth, same geography, same customer. 2 companies can share an industry code and have nothing in common economically.

A sector screen isn't a comp set. It's 40 tickers.

Then pick the multiple the capital structure allows. EV/EBITDA ignores use, so it compares companies financed completely differently. P/E sits after interest, so debt contaminates it, and you should use it only where use genuinely matches.

Calendarise. A December filer and a June filer are 6 months apart.

Nobody mentions it until a VP does.

Use the median, never the mean. 1 outlier drags an average in a set of 8, so show the quartile range too and let the reader see how tight it actually is.

And explain your outliers rather than deleting them. The name trading at twice the median is the most interesting thing in the set.
