A company is sold in bankruptcy for $500. It owes $400 of senior debt and $200 of subordinated debt. Who gets what?

Claims are paid in order. Senior first, then junior, then equity, and each class waits until the one above is paid in full.

Senior gets all 400. That's a 100% recovery.

The $100 left goes to subordinated lenders. 100 out of 200 is a 50% recovery.

Shareholders get 0.

That's also why junior debt pays a higher interest rate. It loses first when value falls.

Save it if you're interviewing for restructuring or credit.
