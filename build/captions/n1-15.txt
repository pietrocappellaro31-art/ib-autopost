$100 of revenue at a software company, or $100 at a grocer. Which would you pay more for?

Drop your pick in the comments first.

Revenue isn't profit. Grocers run on thin margins and software on high ones, so the same 100 of sales turns into very different cash for you as the buyer.

Growth moves the multiple. You're paying today for cash that arrives in year 3, 5, 10.

Durability too.

Subscription revenue that renews is far easier to forecast than sales you have to win again every year.

So I'd usually take software. But a slow, loss-making software company can be worth less than a steady grocer, so say "it depends", then say on what.
