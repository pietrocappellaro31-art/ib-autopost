1 number on that income statement is wrong. Did you find it in 10 seconds?

If you didn't, here's the walk-through.

Depreciation goes up by 10. EBIT falls by 10, from 40 to 30.

But tax is 25% of EBIT, so tax falls too, from 10 to 7.5. That's a 2.5 saving, and it's real cash the company keeps.

So net income falls by 7.5, not 10. It should read 22.5, not 20.

That 2.5 is the tax shield, and it's the whole reason interviewers ask this question. They want to see if you remember that a non-cash expense still saves real tax.
