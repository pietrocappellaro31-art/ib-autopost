"Where does your beta come from?" It's 4 steps, and you'll be asked for all of them.

Pull the levered betas for your comps.

Each one mixes business risk with that company's own debt, so you unlever them: levered beta over 1 + (1 - tax rate) x D/E. A beta of 1.2 with D/E of 0.5 and a 25% tax rate gives 1.2 / 1.375, about 0.87. Use market values for D/E, not book.

Take the median.

Relever it at the target's own structure. At a D/E of 0.3, 0.87 becomes about 1.07, and that's the number that goes into CAPM.

Save this with your WACC notes.
