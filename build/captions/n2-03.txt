"What's your greatest weakness?" The perfectionist answer doesn't sound safe. It sounds like you'd hide a mistake.

Pick something real.

It shouldn't be core to the job, though. Speaking up in a room of 12 people works. Being sloppy with numbers, for an analyst role, doesn't.

Then 3 beats: the weakness in 1 sentence, 1 time it cost you, and what you changed.

Spend most of your 2 minutes on that last part, because they're listening for self-awareness and action.

And don't say it's gone. Say what habit you use now.

Which weakness would you pick?
