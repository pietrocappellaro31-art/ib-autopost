You buy one unit for $10, then one for $12. You sell one for $20. Is your profit $8 or $10?

Both. It depends which unit you say you sold, and that's your inventory method.

First in, first out (FIFO): the $10 unit goes first, so profit is $10 and the $12 unit stays in inventory.

Last in, first out (LIFO): the $12 unit goes first, so profit is $8 and the $10 unit stays.

When prices are rising, LIFO shows less profit and so less tax. At a 25% rate that's 50 cents less on this one sale. US GAAP allows LIFO. IFRS doesn't.

Now flip it: prices fall, $10 then $8. Which method shows more profit? Tell me in the comments.
