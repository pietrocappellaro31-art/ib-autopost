Can goodwill ever be negative? That's level 4. Start at level 1 and keep score.

Level 1: assets = liabilities + equity. Everything a company owns was paid for by someone, either lenders or owners.

Level 2: nothing. Cash goes down $50, inventory goes up $50, so total assets don't move. You swapped one asset for another.

Level 3: $200. Goodwill is the price paid above the fair value of the net assets you buy: $500 - $300.

Level 4: not on the balance sheet. If you pay less than the net assets are worth, you check the values again and then book the difference as a gain straight away. It's called a bargain purchase.
