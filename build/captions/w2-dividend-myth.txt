A $1 dividend lands in your account. Your $50 share drops to $49. So did you get paid?

Not really. Part of what made the share worth $50 was the company's cash. When $1 of that cash goes out to you, the company is worth $1 a share less, so in theory the price falls by about $1 on the ex-dividend day.

You end up with a $49 share and $1 of cash. That's $50, the same as before. It's cash moving from one pocket to the other, and if that dollar gets taxed you're a little behind.

Price before = price after + the dividend. A dividend moves value to you, it doesn't make any.
