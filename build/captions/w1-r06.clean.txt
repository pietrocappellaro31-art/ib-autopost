8 words that tell an interviewer you haven't done the reading.

"Profit." Net income, EBIT or EBITDA? That's 3 different numbers leading 3 different places.

"Valuation." Enterprise value or equity value? They differ by net debt, and the entire bridge exists because of it.

"Cash flow." Operating, levered or unlevered? Each takes its own discount rate.

"Debt." Gross or net?

"Multiple." Of what, over which period, forward or trailing? 12 times earnings means nothing on its own.

None of this is vocabulary for its own sake. Precision is the cheapest signal you can send in a first round, and it costs you 1 extra word each time you open your mouth.

Say which one you mean. Every time.
