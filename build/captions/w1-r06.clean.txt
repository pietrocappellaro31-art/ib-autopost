8 words that tell an interviewer you haven't done the reading.

"Profit." Net income, EBIT or EBITDA? 3 different lines on the income statement. Name one.

"Valuation." Enterprise value or equity value? The bridge between them is a question you'll get on its own.

"Cash flow." Operating, levered or unlevered? If it's levered, you discount at the cost of equity. If it's unlevered, you use WACC.

"Debt." Gross or net? Net is what's left after you take off the cash.

"Multiple." Of what, and LTM or forward? 12 times means nothing on its own.

"Margin." Gross, EBITDA or net? Same revenue, 3 very different percentages.

"Shares." Basic or diluted? You'll want diluted for equity value.

"Return." IRR or MOIC? IRR cares how long it took. MOIC doesn't.

None of this is vocabulary for its own sake. Precision is the cheapest signal you can send in a first round, and it costs you exactly 1 extra word every time you open your mouth to answer a technical.

Say which one you mean. Every time.
