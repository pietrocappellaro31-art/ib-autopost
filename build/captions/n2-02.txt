Unlevered or levered free cash flow? It comes down to 1 line: interest.

Unlevered is before interest. EBIT of 100 taxed at 25% is 75, plus D&A of 20, less capex of 30, less a $10 rise in working capital. That's 55, and it belongs to lenders and shareholders.

Levered is after interest. Take off 20 of interest after tax, which is 15, and you're at 40. That's what's left for shareholders.

Then match the rate. Unlevered at WACC gives you enterprise value. Levered at the cost of equity gives you equity value.

The classic slip is discounting levered FCF at WACC.
