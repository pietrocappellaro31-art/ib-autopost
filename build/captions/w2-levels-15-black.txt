Does paying back a loan lower net income?

A lot of people say yes. Money leaves the company, so profit must drop. It doesn't.

Interest is the expense. Borrow $100 at 10% with a 25% tax rate and net income falls by $7.50 a year: $10 of interest, less $2.50 of tax saved. The $100 you hand back is the loan itself, so cash and debt both drop by $100 and the income statement never sees it. You'll find it in financing cash flow.

That split between interest and principal is what the question is testing.
