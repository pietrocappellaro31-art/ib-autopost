"Tell me about a time you worked in a team." Say "we" the whole way through and they learn nothing about you.

Pick a team with real stakes. A deadline that could actually be missed, a client or a professor who'd notice.

Then it's "I" for your part. The goal, your role, what you did. Keep "we" for the outcome.

Put the friction in.

A missed handoff, a disagreement on the approach, someone falling 2 days behind, and how you dealt with it without turning anyone into the villain. Finish on what happened and 1 thing you'd repeat.

Deal teams are small. They're picturing you on theirs.
