Accretive or dilutive, in your head, in 5 seconds.

All-stock deal. Compare the 2 price-to-earnings multiples.

Acquirer trades at 20 times earnings. Target at 15 times at the offer price. The acquirer is buying earnings more cheaply than its own are valued, so it's accretive.

Flip it. Pay 25 times for something when you trade at 20 and it's dilutive, because you're issuing cheap shares to buy expensive earnings.

That's the rule. It gets you the direction.

Now say what it ignores, because this is the part that gets you the offer. Synergies. Transaction fees. The financing mix, since paying with cash or debt changes the maths completely. New amortisation from the purchase price allocation.

5 seconds gets you the direction. Naming the 4 things the rule ignores is what makes you sound like you've run the model.
