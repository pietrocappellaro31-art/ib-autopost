Depreciation goes up $10. What happens to cash?

Most people say it drops $10. Nope. It goes up $2.50.

Here's why. Profit before tax falls $10, and at a 25% tax rate you pay $2.50 less tax, so net income only falls $7.50. But depreciation was never a payment, so the cash flow statement adds the whole $10 back.

-7.50 + 10 = +2.50.

The only cash that moved? The tax you didn't pay.

(Usual interview set-up: 25% tax, tax follows the books, nothing else changes.)
