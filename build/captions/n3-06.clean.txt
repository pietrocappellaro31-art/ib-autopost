"Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" Say "private equity" and they hear that you're leaving in 2.

They're checking 1 thing.

Will you stay long enough, and work hard enough, to be worth the time it takes to train you? So start with the analyst years: learning the modelling, getting on live deals, being trusted with a bit more each quarter.

Then talk skills, not titles. Judgement on deals beats "MD by 30."

Keep it to 3 sentences. It's a quick check, not a life plan.

What would you say if they asked you tomorrow?
