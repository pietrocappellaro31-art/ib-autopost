Most people say yes. She isn't. She ends the week at $75.

Her stock starts at $100. Monday it drops 50%, so half of $100 is gone and she has $50 left. Friday it climbs 50%, but that's 50% of the $50 she has now, not of the $100 she started with. Only $25 comes back.

$50 + $25 = $75.

To get from $50 back to $100 she'd need the $50 to double. That's a 100% gain, not 50%.

A percent move is always measured from where the price is now.
