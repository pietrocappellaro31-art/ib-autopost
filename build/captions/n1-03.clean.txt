"Why investment banking?" Try this test on your answer: would it work just as well for consulting?

"A steep learning curve" fits consulting. "Smart people" fits law. "Passion for finance" fits nothing in particular. None of it's wrong. It just doesn't answer the question.

Build it in 3 beats instead. 1 moment that pulled you toward deals. 1 thing you actually did about it. Then why the analyst job itself is the next step, the modelling, the live transactions, seeing a deal from pitch to close.

Leave exit options out. They hear "I want to leave in 2 years."

Then say it out loud with a timer running. Under 60 seconds.

Which line in your current answer would fail the test?
