How do you value a company with 2 very different businesses? You don't use 1 multiple.

You do a sum of the parts, valuing each division against its own peers.

Division A: EBITDA of 50 at 8 times is 400. Division B: EBITDA of 30 at 12 times is 360. That's an enterprise value of 760.

Debt and cash usually sit at group level, so you take net debt of 200 off once, at the end. Equity value: 560.

Head office costs don't belong to either division. Value them and subtract them.

If the market values the group below the sum, that's the conglomerate discount, and it's why spin-offs happen.
