"Tell me about a deal you've followed." Most candidates recite the headline. Interviewers want your view.

Pick 1 deal you can talk about for 10 minutes, ideally in the group you're interviewing for.

Know the buyer, the target, the price and how it was paid, from the announcement or the filing.

Explain why the buyer wanted it, why the seller sold, and why now. Most people only cover the buyer.

Have the multiple paid and how it compares with peers.

Then say whether it's a good deal and name the biggest risk.

Which deal would you use?
