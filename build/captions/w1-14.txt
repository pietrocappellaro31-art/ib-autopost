Somebody gave you 15 minutes. Most people waste 11 of them talking about themselves.

The first 60 seconds: thank them, name whoever connected you, 2 lines on you, and 1 on why you asked for this call specifically. Then stop talking.

Ask what only they can answer. "What's banking like" is a brochure question and you'll get a brochure answer. "How did you end up in this group" and "what surprised you in year 1" are questions nobody else can answer for them.

Show the reading without performing it. 1 specific reference, not 3. "I saw you worked on that deal, what was hard about it?" One is interest. Three is research, and it reads differently.

Minute 13 is the entire call. 2 questions: is there anyone else in the group worth speaking to, and is there anything I should be doing now. Both are easy to say yes to, and 1 of them hands you the next conversation.

Then follow up inside 24 hours, referencing something specific they said so it's obviously not a template.

Save this before your next one.
