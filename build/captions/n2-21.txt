Your interview thank-you note is the last thing they read from you. Most are generic.

Send it the same day, within 24 hours, by email.

1 note per interviewer. A group email reads like a template.

3 sentences: thanks for their time, 1 specific thing you discussed, and why it confirmed your interest. Leave the full pitch out.

Then check the name and the bank twice. A wrong bank name from your last template undoes a good interview.

Save it for after your next superday.
