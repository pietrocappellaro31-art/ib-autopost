A 2-for-1 split doubles your shares. So are you any richer? Nope.

Say you own 100 shares at $50. That's $5,000. After the split you hold 200 shares, but each one is now half as much of the company, so the price drops to $25. 200 x $25 is still $5,000.

Nothing about the business changed. Same profit, same cash. Earnings per share go from $2.50 to $1.25, so the stock still trades at 20x.

Think of it like a pizza cut into 16 slices instead of 8. You've got more slices, but your piece is exactly the same size. Even the dividend follows: $1 a share becomes 50 cents, on twice the shares.
