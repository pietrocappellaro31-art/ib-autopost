"Walk me through a merger model." 5 steps, in order, and the follow-ups get easier.

1. Price: the target's share price plus a premium, times its diluted shares.
2. Funding: cash, new debt or new stock. Each has a cost: interest you give up, interest you pay, or more shares.
3. Combine: buyer net income plus target net income, plus synergies, less funding costs and new D&A from write-ups. All after tax.
4. New share count.
5. Pro forma EPS against the buyer's standalone EPS. Up is accretive, down is dilutive.

Then say what it means for value, not just EPS.

Save it for the M&A round.
