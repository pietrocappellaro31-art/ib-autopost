"Any questions for me?" Not the end of the interview. The last part of it.

Ask about their work. "Which deal taught you the most?" gets a real answer.

"What's a typical day like?" gets you the website.

Then ask what the group rewards, something like "what separates analysts who do well here?", because it tells you how they actually judge people and it shows them you're already thinking about being good at the job rather than just getting the offer.

Close the loop. "Is there anything in my background I can clarify?" is your 1 last shot at fixing a weak answer.

Prepare 4. Ask 2. Skip pay and hours.
