"Any questions for me?" You get 1 shot to sound like you actually want the seat.

Not "what's the culture like." You'll get the identical answer in all 8 interviews you sit this cycle, and they know it.

4 that land:

"What does the first 6 months look like for someone who's doing well?" This tells you what they measure.

"What's the last deal you worked on that you'd actually want to talk about?" People light up, and you find out what the group really does all day rather than what the website says.

"What separates the analyst you keep from the one you don't?" You're asking for the bar, directly.

"What's changed here in the last year?" Harder to answer from a script.

Then 1 more that only works if you did the reading. Something they wrote, a deal they ran, a sector they moved into.

A question about something they personally did is the only one they remember.
