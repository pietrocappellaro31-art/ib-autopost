5 answers that get you dinged.

"A DCF, because it's the most accurate." It isn't. It's the most assumption-heavy method you have, and 3 inputs nobody can verify drive the entire answer. Say that instead and you sound like someone who's built one.

"Add cash to get enterprise value." You subtract it. This one ends interviews.

"Depreciation goes up 10, so net income falls 10." It falls 7.5 at a 25% tax rate, because the tax shield is real cash you keep. The whole question exists to test that.

"Goodwill is the premium paid." Close. It's purchase price less the fair value of net identifiable assets acquired.

"No, I think you covered everything." After 30 minutes, that's the cheapest possible way to look uninterested.

Every one of these has a better version. Each costs you a single extra sentence.

Save this for the night before.
