5 answers that get you dinged, and the 1 sentence that fixes each.

"A DCF, because it's the most accurate." It isn't. It's the most assumption-heavy method you have, because growth, margins, WACC and terminal value are all your inputs. Say that and you sound like someone who's built one.

"Add cash to get enterprise value." You subtract it. Cash comes with the company, so it lowers what you're really paying.

"Depreciation goes up 10, so net income falls 10." It falls 7.5 at a 25% tax rate. The tax shield is real, and that's the whole point of the question.

"Goodwill is the premium paid." Close. It's the purchase price minus the fair value of the net identifiable assets you acquire.

"No, I think you covered everything." Always bring 1 question about something they actually worked on.

Each fix costs you a single extra sentence.

Save this for the night before.
