Maintenance or incurrence covenant? It's about when the test runs.

Maintenance covenants get tested every period, often every 3 months. Say the limit is 4 times debt / EBITDA, with 350 of debt. EBITDA falls from 100 to 75, leverage goes from 3.5 to about 4.7 times, and you've breached it without doing a thing. You'll see these in bank loans.

Incurrence covenants only run when you want to act, like raising more debt or paying a dividend. They're common in high-yield bonds.

A breach usually ends in a waiver or an amendment, for a fee or a higher rate. Not bankruptcy.

Covenant-lite loans drop the maintenance tests and keep the incurrence ones.
