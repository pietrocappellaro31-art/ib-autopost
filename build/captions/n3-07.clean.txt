How do you value a company that loses money? P/E is a price divided by a loss. It means nothing.

So you move up the income statement: EV/EBITDA if EBITDA's positive, EV/Revenue if it isn't.

If it's expected to turn a profit in 2 or 3 years, use forward numbers.

Stretch the DCF too.

A 5-year forecast that ends while the company's still losing money won't tell you much, so you keep forecasting until margins settle and only then do the terminal value.

And check the funding. If it's burning cash it may need to raise more, which dilutes today's owners.
