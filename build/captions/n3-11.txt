Should the buyer pay in cash or stock? There's no single right answer. There's a right way to reason, and it takes about 4 steps.

1. Cash makes sense when the buyer has it, or can borrow cheaply, and it's confident in the target. Its own shareholders keep all the upside.

2. Stock makes sense when the buyer's shares look rich, the deal's too big for cash, or there's doubt about the target. The seller becomes an owner and shares the risk. You pay for that in dilution.

3. Ask what the seller wants. Cash is certain. Stock is a slice of what comes next.

4. Weigh the cost first, then who should carry the risk.
