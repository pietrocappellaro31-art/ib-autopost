# YouTube #13 visual plan: GameStop, January 2021 (written before the render)

Film: "109% of GameStop Was Sold Short. How Is That Possible?" Score: `suspense` (`score.py`), style noir jazz in D
(today is the transition day of `score.pick`'s daily slots: see the episode). Narrator HEART (af_heart, 0.9). Camera
`auto` on drawn visuals, keyed cine cameras on filmed beats. Sound: action-timed in the drawn beats (`fx="action"`).

**Opening minute (Pietro, 18 Nov, first time):** `hook_first=True` (new engine option): the film opens on the filmed
hook, chapter 1's card follows it; 109% counts on the phone from frame 0 (on screen at 0.2 s, final at 1.4 s); the
question "How can you sell more of something than exists?" is said by about 0:11. The title and both thumbnails carry
109%. The puzzle that can stand alone as a Short: the lending chain (chapter 3), maths that holds in any country.

**First job (the 18 Nov next improvement): score separation.** `score.pick` now picks by day and format slot (four
different styles and keys a day, none of yesterday's four) and writes its choice to library.json at pick time;
`score.STYLE_LEVEL` holds a measured music level per style so the voice sits near 24 dB over every style
(`build/score_levels.py`). **Then build order step 3 (sets): a city street today** (`cine_2021.street_today`, night
and day) plus a kitchen and an operations room at dawn; step 4 (data in the world): a billboard that counts the same
shares sold twice, a laptop that lands sourced prices on a true time axis.

Seven filmed beats: six on the new 2021 sets and one filmed document. Every reconstruction badged ("not the real
office", "our illustration · not a real app"); nobody on screen is a portrait or speaks; no app, broker, fund or
company logo is drawn.

Sources (keys as in the episode docstring): SEC, TEN, T1, FOX. Checks: `build/plans/ep013_checks.py`.
