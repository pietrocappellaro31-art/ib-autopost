# YouTube #9 visual plan: Ponzi and the reply coupon, 1920 (written before the render)

Film: "Ponzi's Scheme Started With a Real Trade. Here's Why It Could Never Work." Score: `suspense` (`score.py`, one
mood per chapter: mystery, decide, explain, decide, crash, resolve across the ten chapters). Narrator HEART (af_heart,
0.9). Camera: `auto` on drawn visuals, keyed cine cameras on filmed beats. Fourteen filmed beats: seven on three new
sets in `build/cine_1920.py` (a Boston street of 1920, a 1920 office with a counter, two post offices with the sea
between) and seven filmed documents from `cine_lib.document_shot` (the 14 Nov next improvement: a quote is read off a
document on a desk, inked as it is said, the key words highlighted as they are read, the camera following the line).
Every reconstruction badged; nobody on screen is a portrait or speaks; the coupon and the stamp are our own designs.

`build/plans/ep009_checks.py`.
