# YouTube #16 visual plan: Enron, 1999 to 2001 (written before the render)

Film: "$27 Billion of Enron Was Off the Books. Its Board Saw the Chart." Score: `suspense` (`score.py`), style, key and
tempo from `score.pick`'s slot for today's long film (see music.json). Narrator HEART (af_heart, 0.9). Camera `auto` on
drawn visuals, keyed cine cameras on filmed beats. Sound: action-timed in the drawn beats (`fx="action"`).

**Opening minute (Pietro, 18 Nov):** `hook_first=True`: the film opens on the filmed hook, the boardroom's wall display
filling the frame, ALL ASSETS $60BN drawn and OFF ITS BOOKS counting $0 to $27BN from frame 0 (final at 1.4 s), ALMOST
50% at 1.6 s; the question ("So why didn't the board stop it?") is the hook's last sentence, said by about 0:11. The
second beat is filmed (the share price on a billboard: $90.75 and $0.26 on a true axis), and so is chapter 2's first
(revenues $40bn to $100bn on an office wall screen), so the first minute is filmed except the chapter-1 map beat.
Title and both thumbnails carry $27BN. The number puzzle that can stand alone as a Short: the hedge paid in your own
shares (our numbers: a $100 investment falls to $60, the partner's $100 of your shares cover the $40; then your shares
fall 70% to $30 and the hedge cannot pay), maths that holds in any country.

**First job (the 22 Nov next improvement):** "make yt_preview list the five quietest 6 s windows with their beats, so the
next run can lift the lowest window above #14's 0.58; then build order step 3: a boardroom today". Built:
`youtube.make` writes `beats.json` (every beat's time, chapter, visuals, first words); `yt_preview.quietest()` keeps the
five quietest non-overlapping 6 s windows and `--motion` names the beat under each (motion.json "quietest"). The film
is written so that no drawn beat is a held drawing beside a line: drawing-beside-text share 32% (#15) to 26% planned,
17 filmed beats (#15 14). Build order step 3: `cine_2001.boardroom_2001` (glass on a city at dusk or by day, a wall
display that takes content, a long table with laptops, papers, water and a speakerphone whose light pulses, chairs, a
wall clock, seated directors who read, look up and react), used by 5 shots. Measured after the render against #15
(lowest 6 s window 0.44, 82 windows under 1.2, static share 4.6%, mean motion 3.74) and #14 (0.58).
