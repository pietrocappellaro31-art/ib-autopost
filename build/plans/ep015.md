filling the frame, LOST BY ONE TRADER counting £0 to £827M from frame 0 (final at 1.4 s), THE BANK'S RECORDED CAPITAL

both thumbnails carry £827M. The number puzzle that can stand alone as a Short: our insurance example (£1 a day for 99

windows under 1.2 against #14". Built: `cine_1995.balance_board` (capital and hidden losses on one true scale, £m to

the render: windows under 1.2, lowest 6 s window, static share, mean motion; variety share 36% (#14) to 32% planned.
