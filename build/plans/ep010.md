# YouTube #10 visual plan: the salad oil swindle, 1963 (written before the render)

Film: "Wall Street Lent Against Tanks of Salad Oil. Most of the Oil Wasn't There." Score: `suspense` (`score.py`, one
mood per chapter from `youtube.SCORE_PLANS`). Narrator HEART (af_heart, 0.9). Camera `auto` on drawn visuals, keyed cine
cameras on filmed beats. Sound: **action-timed in the drawn beats** (`fx="action"`, the 15 Nov next improvement): every
drawing and HTML visual reports its actions (stamp lands, coin lands, bar finishes, marker passage, paper, card pops)
and gets the matching sound at that moment; no generic pop per visual, no ding per card (a low mallet as the title
lands, plus the card drawing's own sounds).

Eleven filmed beats: seven on a new 1963 world in `build/cine_1963.py` (an oil tank yard by the water at dusk and at
night with a tank that can be cut away to show water, oil or nothing; a receipt's journey from the yard to a field
warehouse office, a broker and a bank; the 1971 dealing room re-dressed for November 1963) and four filmed documents
(`cine_lib.document_shot`). Every reconstruction badged; nobody on screen is a portrait or speaks; the receipt is our
design with every figure greeked.

Sources (keys as in the episode docstring): TIME65, TIME63, TIMEAX, BUNGE, DEANG, NBER, INVNEWS, SECHS, PULITZER,
FORTUNE, SIPC. Checks: `build/plans/ep010_checks.py`.
