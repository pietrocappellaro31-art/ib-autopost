# YouTube #11 visual plan: Herstatt, 26 June 1974 (written before the render)

Film: "A Bank Shut at 3:30. The Banks That Had Already Paid It Never Got Their Dollars." Score: `suspense` (`score.py`,
style, key and tempo from `score.pick`: never one of the last three videos'). Narrator HEART (af_heart, 0.9). Camera
`auto` on drawn visuals, keyed cine cameras on filmed beats. Sound: action-timed in the drawn beats (`fx="action"`).

**New today: held life** (`held_life=True`, `sketch.held_life`): once a drawing has finished drawing itself, every
part of it boils (re-drawn a little off its place six times a second, independently, like hand-drawn animation) and
the whole drawing sways a fraction of a degree about its base. Measured before and after on #10's drawn spans
(`build/held_life_check.py`).

Seven filmed beats: five on a new 1974 world in `build/cine_1974.py` (a payments room with two moving wall clocks and a
telex; two bank counters, Frankfurt and New York, either side of the sea, each with its own clock and window light;
a New York correspondent's account desk with a ledger, phone and safe; the 1971 dealing room re-dressed for 1974; a
settlement desk between the two counters) and two filmed documents (`cine_lib.document_shot`). Every reconstruction
badged; nobody on screen is a portrait or speaks; no bank's name, logo or building is copied (the signs read
"BANK · FRANKFURT", "BANK · NEW YORK").

Sources (keys as in the episode docstring): BIS26, GALATI, NORMAN, BCBS, FINVIA, CLS24, TRI25. Checks:
`build/plans/ep011_checks.py`. New drawing: `sketch.day_bars` (two banking days five hours apart on one German clock,
illustrative hours, labelled; stage 4 is the sourced CLS window 07:00 to 12:00 CET).
