# YouTube #12 visual plan: Long-Term Capital Management, 1998 (written before the render)

Film: "Two Nobel Winners Ran This Fund. It Lost Half Its Money in a Month." Score: `suspense` (`score.py`; style, key
and tempo from `score.pick`, never one of the last three videos'). Narrator HEART (af_heart, 0.9). Camera `auto` on
drawn visuals, keyed cine cameras on filmed beats. Sound: action-timed in the drawn beats (`fx="action"`).

**New today (the 17 Nov next improvement): held life for the HTML text visuals** (`text_life=True`,
`youtube.TEXT_LIFE`, motion.JS fx `idle`). Number, compare, list, flow, timeline, term, icon, icons, verdict, split,
quote and statement cards used to freeze once their entrance ended; now each card or item, once landed, bobs a few
px and tilts a fraction of a degree on its own period and phase (compare cards out of step, list items in turn).
Measured on this film's own text spans with `python3 build/held_life_check.py --text build/youtube_ep012.py ...`.

**Also new:** `build/cine_1998.py`, the first late-1990s rooms (build order step 3): an open-plan trading room with
beige CRT monitors whose screens live (greeked rows that change, a yield screen) and a split-flap board; a panelled
meeting room at night with a moving wall clock, a long table and an invented skyline. People step (step 2):
`cine.sit_keys` / `cine.stand_keys`, sitting down as one motion (lean over the knees, drop, straighten) instead of a
cut between poses; used by the bankers who arrive in `fed_evening`. Data in the world (step 4): the CAPITAL row counts
4.8, 4.1, 2.3 on the words; the yield screen's gap closes (our illustration) and later opens.

Nine filmed beats: five on the new 1998 sets and four filmed documents (`cine_lib.document_shot`). Every
reconstruction badged ("not the real office", "not the real room"); nobody on screen is a portrait or speaks; no
firm's name, logo or building is drawn (the board says FUND CAPITAL; the term sheet says TERM SHEET and three sourced
terms only).

Sources (keys as in the episode docstring): PWG, MCD, FRH, GRN, MAC, NOB, FUR. Checks: `build/plans/ep012_checks.py`.
