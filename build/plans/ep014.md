and tempo from `score.pick`'s slot for today's long film (see music.json). Narrator HEART (af_heart, 0.9). Camera

**Opening minute (Pietro, 18 Nov):** `hook_first=True`: the film opens on the filmed hook, OVER $40BN counting on a
phone from frame 0 (final at 1.4 s), IN ONE DAY at 1.6 s; the question ("So how does a bank full of bonds go bust in
two days?") is the hook's last sentence, said by about 0:13. The second beat is filmed too (the 19 Nov next
improvement: "film the second beat too ... so the whole first minute moves"): the bank office's wall screen draws
the $15bn gap. Title and both thumbnails carry $40 billion. The number puzzle that can stand alone as a Short: the
$100 bond at 1.5% priced at $79.72 once new bonds pay 4% (chapter 3), maths that holds in any country.

**First job (the 19 Nov next improvement):** (1) the second beat filmed; (2) `yt_preview.py` fails on any static
second in the first 60 s (`--motion` and `--clip` exit 2, `opening_static_s` in motion.json) and the variety test
fails a `hook_first` film whose first beat is not filmed. **Then build order step 3 (sets): a bank office today**
(`cine_2023.bank_office_today`: open plan, window on the city, wall screen that takes content, glass meeting room,
back row of live monitors, front desk with a monitor and a paper) and a **bank branch front today**
(`branch_front_today`: fascia BANK only, glass doors with a notice slot, cash machine); step 4 (data in the world):
wall-screen bars on a true dollar scale that grow as they are said, a desk monitor where a rate and a price count
against each other.

Eight filmed beats: seven on the new sets (and the 2021 kitchen and street) and one filmed document. Every
reconstruction badged; nobody on screen is a portrait or speaks; no bank's name on a building, no logo.

Sources (keys as in the episode docstring): FED, BAR, 10K, JS, FDIC, AB. Checks: `build/plans/ep014_checks.py`.
