# shorts-four-percent-wipeout (YouTube Short, Format D: an animated calculation)

- Platform: YouTube Short, uploaded by hand by Pietro. Not cross-posted to Instagram.
- Cut from: YouTube #12, "Two Nobel Winners Ran This Fund. It Lost Half Its Money in a Month." (yt-012-ltcm, 18 Nov); set as the Short's Related video in Studio.
- Concept: $100 of bonds bought with $4 of your own and $96 borrowed (25 to 1). A 4% fall takes all $4 (the loan does not shrink); a 1% fall takes a quarter. Every move is 25 times as large on your own money. LTCM ran at more than 25 to 1 and lost 44% of its capital in August 1998. The maths holds in any country and currency.
- Sources checked in the run (19 Nov 2026, all opened again): PWG report, April 1999 (CFTC copy: "a balance-sheet leverage ratio of more than 25-to-1"; $4.1bn capital on 31 July; $1.8bn August losses; $2.3bn left; about $3.6bn from 14 firms); Federal Reserve History, Near Failure of LTCM ("lost 44 percent of its value in August alone"; 14 firms, $3.6bn; two Nobel laureates); McDonough statement, 1 Oct 1998 (14 firms; "Not one penny of public money was spent or committed."). Links in the description and the episode docstring.
- Our arithmetic (episode docstring): 100 = 96 + 4; 100 / 4 = 25; 4% x 100 = 4; 96 - 96 = 0; 1 / 4 = 25%; 1.8 / 4.1 = 43.9% (said 44%, as Federal Reserve History). The $100 example is labelled ours in the description, never presented as one of LTCM's trades.
- Script: ours (build/shorts_four-percent-wipeout.py). No reconstruction, no real person drawn or named on screen (only the fund's initials, LTCM, as a label).
- Voice: HEART, synthetic (Kokoro af_heart, Apache 2.0, en-us, speed 0.9). Imitates nobody. No new PHONEMES (ltcm added 18 Nov).
- Masters: one delivered file, scored (Pietro, 17 Nov). The clean mix stays in build/out as the score gate's reference only.

No commercial or platform-library music in the file. Credit line: "Music: original score made for this video."

Drawings in build/sketch.py (lv_* scenes): bonds and loan columns to scale, a coin stack, a 5 x 5 bond grid, LTCM's capital bar and balance sheet to scale, two trade tickets and gauges, our trader character. No fund or bank logo, no photo.
