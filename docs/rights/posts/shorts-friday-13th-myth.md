# shorts-friday-13th-myth (YouTube Short, Format E: myth)

- Platform: YouTube Short, uploaded by hand by Pietro. Not cross-posted to Instagram. Replaces shorts-friday-13th-stocks as the day's Short.
- Concept: the Friday the 13th stock market curse checked: a 1987 US study (Kolb and Rodriguez) found lower returns; other studies did not confirm it; a UK study of the FT 30 from July 1935 to December 1994 (almost 60 years) found Friday the 13th returns, if anything, higher; bonus, Dutch insurers' averages of 7,500 road accidents on Friday the 13th against 7,800 on other Fridays. Verdict: a myth on this evidence; "superstition isn't a strategy" is opinion, not advice.
- Sources checked: Dumitriu and Stefanescu, MPRA 95296 (2019) on Kolb and Rodriguez (1987); Coutts, Applied Economics Letters 6(1), 1999; DutchNews.nl, 12 Jun 2008. Links in the description and the episode docstring.
- Script: ours (build/shorts_friday-13th-myth.py), told by one recurring character of our own design (the trader).
- Voice: HEART, synthetic (Kokoro af_heart, Apache 2.0, en-us, speed 0.9). Imitates nobody.
- Music: none in the master. Pietro may add a sound in the YouTube app at upload from YouTube's licensed Shorts library (suggestions: Pietro's library pick, REFERENCE_ONLY, plus trending sounds).
- Score: a second, scored master (shorts-friday-13th-myth-scored.mp4) carries an original score written by build/score.py (moods mystery, discover, resolve; F major, 80 bpm, seed 7), rendered with the GeneralUser GS SoundFont (free for commercial music). Rights class ORIGINAL_GENERATED: allowed in the file on any platform; no attribution needed. The standard includes the music"); not yet heard by anyone.
- Sound effects: synthesised in build/sfx.py. No whoosh.

Drawings in build/sketch.py: the trader, calendar pages, a phone, research papers, magnifiers, a timeline with a year counter, a trading screen, a road with a car and a black cat, a MYTH stamp. The two bar charts show only the direction each study found and are labelled "direction only, not to scale" and "illustration, not to scale". No real person shown or voiced, no logos, no photos.
