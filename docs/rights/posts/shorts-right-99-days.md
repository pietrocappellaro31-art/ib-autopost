# shorts-right-99-days (YouTube Short, Format C: one surprising concept, built as a calculation)

- Platform: YouTube Short, uploaded by hand by Pietro. Not cross-posted to Instagram.
- Cut from: YouTube #15, "One Trader Lost £827 Million. His Bank Had About £350 Million." (yt-015-barings-827-million, 22 Nov); set as the Short's Related video in Studio.
- Concept: you sell insurance against a big market move and collect £1 on each calm day; 99 calm days = +£99; day 100 the market jumps and you pay £150; 99 wins and one loss, down £51. How often you win is not enough; the size of the loss when you're wrong decides it. Selling options "can" work like this (a general description, no figure on it). Close (not answered): win £1 on 90 days of 100, lose £10 on the other 10 (behind, -£10). The maths holds in any country and currency.
- Sources: none needed on screen; the numbers are the film's own labelled example and our arithmetic (build/plans/ep015_checks.py, the episode docstring). The film's Barings pages (Hansard, RBA Bulletin, NLB Infopedia) were asked for first, before writing, and could not be re-opened (fetch permission unanswered while unattended), so the Short was planned from the start without any Barings figure or link; Barings is named only as the film's subject, on the close chip and in the description's pointer to the film.
