# w2-two-ways-to-pay (viral reference proposal 1: "Same $100 company. Two ways to pay.")

- Platform: Instagram Reel, posted by hand by Pietro. 12 s loop, no voice.
- Concept: the same company bought for $100 and sold for $150; all equity gives 1.5x (150 / 100), $40 equity + $60 debt gives 2.25x ((150 - 60) / 40). On screen and in the caption: debt cuts both ways (sold for $80: 0.8x against 0.5x), and interest is left out, said in the caption. Illustrative round numbers; no real deal, firm or outcome claimed.
- Structure: borrowed from a reference Reel Pietro sent on 11 Nov (R2 in docs/daily/viral-references.md): split screen, the same figure in two panels, counters moving together, a moral line at the end, a loop. None of its footage, audio, characters or wording is used.
- Visuals and fonts: ours, HTML and SVG drawn in build/reels_two-ways-to-pay.py. Source Serif 4, IBM Plex Sans (SIL OFL). No stock, logos or AI images.
- Voice: none. Sound effects: synthesised in build/sfx.py, mastered at -24 LUFS (effects only). No music in the master; Pietro adds music in the app (the reference carries a strong beat, about 100 BPM).
- Disclosure: no AI voice or AI imagery, so no AI label or narration line needed.
- Claims: no statistics, no outcomes, no bank named.
- Approval: Pietro posts by hand after reviewing the finished file.
