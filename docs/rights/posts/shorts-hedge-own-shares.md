# shorts-hedge-own-shares (YouTube Short, Format D: an animated calculation)

- Platform: YouTube Short, uploaded by hand by Pietro. Not cross-posted to Instagram.
- Cut from: YouTube #16, "$27 Billion of Enron Was Off the Books. Its Board Saw the Chart." (yt-016-enron-27-billion, 23 Nov); set as the Short's Related video in Studio.
- Concept: your $100 investment is insured against any fall by a partner whose only money is $100 of your own shares. The investment falls to $60: owed $40, the partner has $100, covered. Your shares fall 70%: the partner's $100 is worth $30; it owes $40, has $30, can't pay, and the $30 is your own stock. A hedge is only as good as the money behind it. Close (not answered): how far can your shares fall before the partner can't pay? (more than 60%, not given). The maths holds in any country and currency.
- Sources: none needed on screen; the numbers are the film's own labelled puzzle and our arithmetic (build/plans/shorts_hedge_checks.py, the episode docstring).
