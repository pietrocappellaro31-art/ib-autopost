# shorts-friday-13th-stocks (YouTube Short, Friday the 13th)

- Platform: YouTube Short, uploaded by hand by Pietro. Not cross-posted to Instagram.
- Concept: Friday the 13th and money in four levels: three Friday the 13ths in 2026 (Feb, Mar, Nov); Dutch insurers' averages of about 7,500 road accidents on Friday the 13th against 7,800 on an ordinary Friday; the $800 to $900 million "lost in business" figure is one phobia expert's estimate; do shares do worse that day? Not reliably: a 1987 US study said yes, later studies did not confirm it, and UK shares 1935 to 1994 did better on Friday the 13th than on other Fridays.
- Sources checked: DutchNews.nl, 12 Jun 2008 (Dutch insurance association); National Geographic, 14 Jun 2014 (Donald Dossey's estimate); Coutts, Applied Economics Letters 1999 (FT 30, 1935-94); Dumitriu and Stefanescu, MPRA 95296, 2019 (on Kolb and Rodriguez 1987).
