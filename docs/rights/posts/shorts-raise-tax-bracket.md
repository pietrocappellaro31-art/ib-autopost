# shorts-raise-tax-bracket (YouTube Short, Format E: a myth)

- Platform: YouTube Short, uploaded by hand by Pietro. Not cross-posted to Instagram.
- Concept: the myth that a raise into a higher tax bracket can leave you with less. US federal income tax comes in layers (2026, single filer, taxable income: 10% to $12,400, 12% to $50,400, 22% above); a raise from $50,400 to $51,400 adds $220 of tax and leaves $780; the whole bill goes from $5,800 to $6,020, about 11.7% of income; so the brackets alone can't make a raise cost you.
- Sources checked in the run (18 Nov 2026): IRS newsroom, 2026 inflation adjustments (Rev. Proc. 2025-32: the seven single-filer thresholds); Tax Foundation, 2026 Tax Brackets, Table 1 (agrees); IRS, Federal income tax rates and brackets ("You pay the higher rate only on the part that's in the new tax bracket."). Links in the description and the episode docstring.
- Our arithmetic (episode docstring, checked with a script): 1,240 + 4,560 = 5,800; + 220 = 6,020; 6,020 / 51,400 = 11.71%; take-home after federal income tax $44,600 and $45,380. Scope stated on screen (US FEDERAL, SINGLE FILER, 2026 (IRS)) and in the description (payroll and state taxes, credits and benefits with income limits are not covered and nothing is claimed about them).
