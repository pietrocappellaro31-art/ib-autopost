# Instagram account setup checklist

Work through this once, in the app. Menu names move between app versions and
regions, so treat the paths as "look for this", not exact taps.

## The honest frame

Settings do not make a page go viral. They can only stop it being suppressed,
and that is worth about twenty minutes. Reach comes from original content, how
fast people like, comment, share and save, and whether the content is eligible
to be recommended at all. Everything below is either removing a self-inflicted
handicap or switching on a distribution path. The content does the rest.

## 1. Account type: Creator, not Business

- Settings, Account type and tools, Switch to professional account, **Creator**.
- **Why Creator:** Creator accounts get the broader audio catalogue for Reels
  and Stories. Business accounts get a narrower selection because of commercial
  licensing. Given Reels are the discovery engine and trending audio matters,
  this is the deciding difference.
- Creator also unlocks Subscriptions at 10k followers, which fits an
  info-product path better than Business's Shopping tools.
- **Scheduling is not a reason to pick Business.** Business Suite schedules for
  Business or Creator accounts; it only excludes personal ones.

## 2. Link a Facebook Page

Required for Business Suite to show the account as a posting destination, even
though nothing will be posted to Facebook.

Two different things are called "linking" and only one works. Accounts Centre,
Add Facebook account links a personal Facebook *profile*; it does not satisfy
Business Suite. Connecting the professional account to a *Page* is the one that
matters: Profile, Edit profile, Public business information, Page.

The Page stays bare. No posts, no audience. It exists only as the anchor.

**Verify** by starting a new post in Business Suite and checking the account
appears as an Instagram destination.

## 3. Account Status, the single most important check

- Settings, Account, **Account Status**.
- It shows whether the account and its content are eligible to be recommended
  to non-followers. If it is not green, nothing else here matters: the page is
  invisible to everyone who does not already follow it.
- It also flags a profile photo or bio that breaks the Recommendation
  Guidelines, which can make the whole account ineligible.
- Re-check monthly, and immediately after any post that gets removed.

## 4. Profile fields

- Name field: Investment Banking Interviews.
- Bio: as locked in `brand-system.md`.
- Category: Education, or nearest. Show the label.
- Contact options: off, or email only.
- The account must be **public**. Only public accounts get recommended.

## 5. Distribution settings to turn on

- **Sharing across profiles:** share posts and Reels to the Facebook Page.
  Free distribution, no extra work.
- **Sharing and reuse:** allow remixes and Story shares. Each is another
  distribution path, and refusing them only narrows reach.
- **Upload at highest quality.** Low-quality Reels are recommended less.
- **Auto-generated captions on Reels:** on. Most people watch muted first.

## 6. What drives reach

- **Original content.** Reposted Reels, visible watermarks and aggregated
  content are recommended less. Everything here is original by construction.
- **Reels of 3 minutes or less** are eligible for recommendation.
- **Keywords in the bio, captions and content**, written as phrases a human
  would type. Search now does more work than hashtags.
- **Collabs.** A Collab post lands on both grids and reaches both audiences,
  and can be recommended in Reels, Explore and Feed. The strongest growth lever
  on this list.
- **Speed of early engagement** in the first hour, which is why replying to
  comments early matters.

## 7. Housekeeping

- Two-factor authentication on, recovery codes saved off the phone.
- Hidden Words: default offensive-word filter on, custom list once spam appears.
- Saved replies for the questions that repeat.
