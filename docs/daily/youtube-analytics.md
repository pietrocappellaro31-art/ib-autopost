- 90% of views are outside the US (or not attributed): US-only hooks (Powerball, the penny, BNPL) travel less than
  universal arithmetic.
- Shorts hold about 30% of their length on average: the first seconds decide everything.
- Long films have almost no impressions to work with yet (3 subscribers); the one viewer who stayed for #10 watched it to
  the end, so the problem looks like discovery, not the film.
