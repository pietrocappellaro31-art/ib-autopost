# Daily runs

Three scheduled tasks make the content every day; posting stays manual.

| Task | Time (New York) | Makes | Log |
|---|---|---|---|
| IG Reels | 8:30am | one voiced Reel, voice HEART from 14 Nov (the G2, BELLA, EMMA rotation ended 13 Nov; history in `build/voices/voice_log.json`) | `ig-reels.md` |
| YouTube long videos | 7:00am | one cinematic long-form documentary (16:9) to `docs/production/long-form-standard.md`, voice HEART, clean and scored MP4 | `youtube-long.md` |
| YouTube Shorts | 7:00am | one Short, voice HEART, to `docs/production/short-form-standard.md` | `youtube-shorts.md` |

Each run sends its files (video, cover or thumbnail, description, comment
question) into its own task's thread in Claude, adds one line to its log here,
and adds what it measured and the one thing it improved to `quality-log.md`.
Topics used and ideas for later are in `topics.md`, so nothing repeats.

From 11 Nov the Reel run also works through `viral-references.md`: one proposal a day modelled on
one of Pietro's six reference Reels, once he has sent the videos (Instagram cannot be fetched from here).
Every Levels hook, Reels and YouTube Shorts, is the question alone: no "That's level 4. Keep score." (Pietro, 11 Nov for Reels, 12 Nov for every video; Shorts from 13 Nov).
