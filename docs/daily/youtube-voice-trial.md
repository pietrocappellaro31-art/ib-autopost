# YouTube voice trial

Pietro, 10 Nov: "From now on for the YouTube videos you will experiment with different voices.
A different one each day, until we find the perfect one." From 11 Nov, each day's long video
and Short share one voice, in this order (`voice_clone.youtube_voice()`); after the last one
the list starts again until Pietro picks. All Kokoro (Apache 2.0), synthetic, nobody real.
The Reel voices (G2, BELLA, EMMA) are left out so the two channels sound different.

Audition, 10 Nov (3 lines from the Shorts and the long video, at the speed below, set for
about 2.8 words a second like GEORGE): all 14 read every word (the one slip on most was "one
to one" written as "1 to 1" or the lender heard as "a firm"); DNSMOS 3.34 to 3.57. Left out:
af_nicole (whispery, DNSMOS 1.94) and am_eric (ran sentences together when slowed).
Baseline: GEORGE (bm_george, every video to 10 Nov), DNSMOS 3.42 on the same lines.
