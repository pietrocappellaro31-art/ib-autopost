## Description

Do stocks really do worse on Friday the 13th?

It's the third Friday the 13th of 2026. We've got 4 questions on the "unluckiest" day for your money, each one harder than the last. Keep score, and tell us yours in the comments.

Level 1: how many Friday the 13ths are there in 2026? (3: February, March and November)
Level 2: Dutch insurers counted road accidents. On a normal Friday the average was 7,800. On Friday the 13th? About 7,500.
Level 3: you've probably seen the $800 to $900 million said to be lost in business every Friday the 13th. That's one phobia expert's estimate.
Level 4: a 1987 US study said shares did worse. But in UK data from 1935 to 1994 they did better on Friday the 13th than on other Fridays. Superstition isn't a strategy.
