# shorts-cent-doubled-30-days (YouTube Short)

## Title

$1 million now, or 1 cent doubled for 30 days? Pick one

## Description

$1 million today, or 1 cent that doubles every day for 30 days? Your gut says take the million. Quiz time: 4 levels, each one harder, so keep score.

Level 1. You put $100 in at 10% a year. After 2 years you've got $121, not $120, because the interest you earned starts earning too.
Level 2. The Rule of 72. Divide 72 by the rate and you get the years it takes to double. At 8%, that's about 9.
Level 3. Did Einstein call compound interest the 8th wonder of the world? There's no proof he ever did. The earliest link to him that Quote Investigator could find is a 1988 newspaper.
Level 4. Take the penny. On day 30 it's worth $5,368,709. On day 20? Still only $5,243. That's the day you'd have quit.

And that's the lesson we keep coming back to. Compounding looks dull for years, then it does nearly all its work at the end, so the hard part isn't the maths. It's staying in.
