# shorts-penny-one-year (YouTube Short)

## Title

The last penny was made a year ago. Which coin loses more?

## Description

A year ago this week, on 12 Nov 2025, the US Mint struck its last circulating penny. So here's a quiz: 4 questions, each one harder. Keep score.

Level 1. What did a penny cost to make? 3.69 cents in fiscal 2024, for a coin worth 1 cent.
Level 2. Your cash total is $4.97 and the shop has no pennies. Under the common rounding rule a 7 rounds down to the nearest 5 cents, so you'd hand over $4.95.
Level 3. Same $4.97, by card? You pay $4.97. Cards aren't rounded.
Level 4. The nickel. It cost 13.78 cents to make a 5 cent coin, so every nickel loses 8.78 cents, while a penny loses 2.69.

Don't throw out the jar at home, though. The penny is still legal tender.
