## Title

Do stocks really do worse on Friday the 13th? We checked

Alternatives: The Friday the 13th stock market curse, checked / Is Friday the 13th bad for stocks? 60 years of data

## Description

Stocks do worse on Friday the 13th. You've heard it, we've all heard it. So we went and checked the studies.

In 1987 a US study (Kolb and Rodriguez) found lower returns on Fridays the 13th. But other researchers couldn't confirm it, and that's where it gets fun. A British study of the FT 30 index from 1935 to 1994, almost 60 years of UK shares, found the opposite: if anything, returns on Friday the 13th were higher than on other Fridays.

Bonus: Dutch insurers counted about 7,500 road accidents on an average Friday the 13th, against 7,800 on other Fridays.

So the curse is a myth, and we'd say superstition isn't a strategy. Would you trade on Friday the 13th? Tell us in the comments.

Quick note: the bar charts in the video show the direction each study found, not their numbers.
