# shorts-singles-day-markup (YouTube Short)

## Title

A price goes up 25%, then 20% off. What do you save?

## Description

It's 11.11, Singles' Day. Students at Nanjing University started it in 1993 as a day for being single, and Alibaba turned it into a sale in 2009 with just 27 shops. Quiz time. 4 levels, each one harder. Keep score.

Level 1. Why 11.11? It's four 1s, four lone sticks.
Level 2. Who made it a sale? Alibaba, in 2009.
Level 3. What has Alibaba stopped publishing since 2022? The total. The last one, for 2021, was 540.3 billion yuan.
Level 4. A price goes up 25%, then it's 20% off. You save nothing. $100 becomes $125, and 20% off $125 is $100 again.

China's regulator told sellers back in 2017 not to raise prices before a discount, which tells you it happens. So when you see a big red percentage this week, check what the thing cost last month.

What did you score? Tell me in the comments.
