# shorts-bnpl-pay-in-four (YouTube Short)

## Title

$1,000 on pay later at 36%. How much do you really owe?

## Description

Black Friday is on 27 November, and a pay later button now sits at nearly every checkout you'll see between now and then. Quiz time. 4 questions on buy now, pay later, each one harder. Keep score.

Level 1. $100 shoes, pay in 4. You hand over $25 at checkout.
Level 2. The other 3 come every 2 weeks, so the shoes are paid off 6 weeks after you buy them.
Level 3. Can it show up on your credit report? Yes. Since 1 April 2025, Affirm reports its Pay in 4 loans to Experian.
Level 4. $1,000 over 12 months at 36% APR, the top of Affirm's range, works out at $100.46 a month. About $1,206 back. That's $206 of interest, more than a fifth of the price.

Pay in 4 at 0% costs nothing extra if you pay on time. The long plans are where the interest lives.

What did you score? Tell me in the comments.
